Who We Are

Precision craftsmanship you can build on

H² Ceiling Engineering was founded in 2023 in Accra and has grown into a trusted name for ceiling, partition and finishing work across Ghana, Togo, Benin and Mali.

We combine modern techniques with careful attention to detail — so every ceiling, wall and finish we deliver looks sharp and lasts for years.

  • Free quotation — no cost, no obligation, tailored to your project.
  • 1–5 year warranty on completed works for your peace of mind.
  • Multilingual team — we work with you in English, French, Twi or Ewe.
  • Wide coverage — Accra, Kumasi, Tamale, Wa, Walewale, Bolga, Togo, Benin and Mali.
